Privacy policy

Calendario web reads and writes the Google Calendar data of its owner's own Google accounts, only to show and edit them on the owner's computer. The data and the access keys stay on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising, and no one but the owner uses the app. The owner can remove its access at any time from his Google account's security settings.

Calendario web's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
